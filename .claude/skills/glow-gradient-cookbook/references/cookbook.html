Glow & Rise

Five techniques behind that page's premium feel — a diagonal gradient, a layered glow shadow, scroll-triggered reveals, a marquee, and a fill bar. Live below each one, with the exact CSS to lift.

Gradients

Three gradients do most of the work

A diagonal blend for hero sections, a vertical vignette that fades dark panels to nothing, and a near-invisible depth gradient on light cards.

Hero diagonal — violet into lavender

Vignette fade — ink to transparent, shown over lavender

Card depth — white into off-white

Glow shadow

A layered box-shadow, not a filter

Four shadows stacked: an inset white top-highlight for glassiness, a solid ring in the deep violet, then two soft colored blooms that read as a glow. Hover intensifies the last two.

Scroll reveal

Fade up, once, on the way into view

Elements start faded and dropped a touch. An IntersectionObserver adds a class that plays a 0.7s ease-out-expo rise, staggered per card. Scroll this section back into view to replay it.

Consistent glow

Every accent — button, chip, bar — draws from the same two violets.

Layered depth

Shadows stack an inset highlight under a colored bloom, never just one blur.

Deliberate motion

Each animation demonstrates the thing it's selling — nothing moves just to move.

Marquee

Loop the content, not the illusion

Duplicate the row's children once, then animate translateX from 0 to -50% on a linear loop. Because the second half is identical to the first, the seam is invisible.

Fill bar

A width animation, triggered on view

Same observer as the scroll reveal, a different target: width goes from 0 to its final value once the bar enters the viewport.
